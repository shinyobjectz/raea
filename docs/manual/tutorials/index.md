Each tutorial is a few minutes long and plays the raea app's own screens, with a narrator explaining each step and a
cursor doing every click. Every page also holds the full narration as text, so you can read it, search it, or hand it
to your agent. Take them in order the first time: each one builds on the one before it.

## Start here

- [Get started with raea](get-started.md): download the app, sign in on raea.io, link your Mac, and land in your first project.
- [Connect your agent](connect-your-agent.md): bring the AI you already pay for, pick the primary that runs your workspace, and make agents of your own.
- [A tour of the app](tour.md): what each part of the sidebar is for, from New chat to Settings.

## Make and review

- [Review work and leave notes](review-and-notes.md): open what your agents made, pin a note to a moment and a place, and get the next version.
- [Share work for review](share-for-review.md): send a cut on a link anyone can watch and comment on, and get their notes back to the agent.
- [Inspect compositions and components](inspect.md): see what a piece is made of, pick any part of the picture, and change it.
- [Controllers](controllers.md): a project's settings, which every agent works to, made by describing them in plain words.
- [Publish](publish.md): connect your accounts and post the approved cut to all of them at once.

## Build on raea

- [Extend raea with the SDK](extend.md): compositions and controllers as TypeScript, the feed's tools, and asking your agent for new work.
- [Work as a team](teams.md): invite your teammates, review together, and share one pool of storage.
- [Libraries: keep, find and share your media](libraries.md): libraries on this Mac or in the cloud, search by what's in a shot, statuses, and projects beside their code.
- [How storage and search work](storage-and-search.md): projects as Git with media in storage the Git LFS way, and searching media by what's in it.
