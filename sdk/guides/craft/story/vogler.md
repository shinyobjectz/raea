# Vogler: the writer's journey

Christopher Vogler's memo on Campbell for Disney's story department (1985), grown into *The Writer's Journey* (1992,
third edition 2007). He put the monomyth into twelve stages in three acts, for screenwriters, and treated the
archetypes as masks: functions a character takes on for a scene, not fixed types. The mentor can turn shadow; the
herald may be a phone call.

In our words: ordinary world, call, refusal, meeting the mentor, crossing the threshold, tests and allies and enemies,
the approach, the ordeal, the reward, the road back, the resurrection (a last test, where the change is proven), and
the return with the elixir.

## The lens (`vogler`)

Stages: `ordinary-world`, `call`, `refusal`, `mentor`, `threshold`, `tests`, `approach`, `ordeal`, `reward`,
`road-back`, `resurrection` (the climax), `return`.

It checks masks by what they do: a `herald` must act in the call, a `shadow` must act against the hero (an event that
turns the focus value down), a hero mask should exist. It finds a missing reward after the ordeal (warn) and a missing
resurrection, a hero never tested a last time (note).

Reach for it when the cast matters: who helps, who opposes, who brings the news.
