# Story: the router

A story in raea is data you write in TypeScript (`raea/story`): who wants what, what happens and why, and how each cut
tells it. raea derives what you'd otherwise argue about (the value curve, what the audience knows at each beat, the
causes, the tension) and checks it before anything is drawn. Read this page first, then the one or two the task needs.

| You're... | Read |
|---|---|
| writing a story for a film, an ad, a game or dialogue | [write](write.md) (`raea guide story/write`) |
| finding out why it doesn't hold, or making `raea check` hold it | [check](check.md) |
| making several versions, or one pattern across many premises | [sample](sample.md) |
| judging a story: lenses, typed questions, the launch shape | [score](score.md) |
| telling a long story (a feature, a game): windows, scenes, story lines | [write](write.md), "Any length" |
| shaping it after a framework | the framework's page, below |

## The frameworks, as lenses

Each is a page in our own words with its sources, and a lens in `raea/story` that maps its stages onto your beats,
says where it breaks and proposes what's missing. Pick by what you're making, not by fame:

| Lens | Reach for it when | Page |
|---|---|---|
| `mckee` | any story with a protagonist and a choice: the default | [mckee](mckee.md) |
| `pixar` | a short with a clear before and after; checking the causal chain | [pixar](pixar.md) |
| `storynomics` | a brand film or ad: the customer is the hero | [storynomics](storynomics.md) |
| `pas` | a 15 to 30 second ad that sells a fix | [pas](pas.md) |
| `campbell` | a journey: leaving, being tested, coming back changed | [campbell](campbell.md) |
| `vogler` | a journey where characters' roles (mentor, shadow, herald) matter | [vogler](vogler.md) |
| `harmon` | episodes, sitcoms, game loops: a circle that comes home | [harmon](harmon.md) |
| `save-the-cat` | a commercial feature or trailer; pacing by position | [save-the-cat](save-the-cat.md) |
| `kishotenketsu` | a piece with no villain: a turn of perspective, not a fight | [kishotenketsu](kishotenketsu.md) |
| `propp` | a folk tale, a quest, a game's mission | [propp](propp.md) |
| `bernays` | any piece that makes a claim: proof shown, never invented | [bernays](bernays.md) |
| `launch` | a product's launch film: trouble, name, it works, proof, start; priors measured on 156 pairs | [launch](launch.md) |

Two or three lenses at most. A story that passes every framework is usually a story no one remembers: the lenses find
holes, they don't write the film.
