# Mode: explainer

A piece whose job is understanding: how something works, why something happens, what a number means. A talking
head with diagrams, a motion graphic, a narrated process.

## The viewer succeeds when
They can explain the one mechanism to someone else afterwards. Not "they saw a lot of information": one idea, held.
The question the viewer arrived with (or the one the first seconds give them) gets answered.

## What ambition looks like
- **One idea per beat.** Each beat adds one step to the viewer's model and nothing else. When a beat holds two
  ideas, it becomes two beats or loses one.
- **Diagrams built, not decorated.** The mechanism drawn as parts that move the way the real thing moves: a flow
  that fills, a comparison that splits, a number that grows from its real starting value to its real end. Build them
  as components; the picture does the explaining and the words point at it.
- **Numbers that mean something.** Every figure is true (BRAND.md or the source said it), shown with its unit, its
  source and a comparison that gives it scale. Animate from true values only.
- **The frame serves the idea.** The direction owns the frame (palette, faces, how diagrams are drawn, one
  signature way of marking what matters); the explanation stays calm inside it. A diagram style the viewer has to
  decode costs the attention the idea needed.

## The rut to name
A wall of bullet text read aloud; stock-footage b-roll that illustrates words literally; icons in circles. The
opposite rut: a beautiful abstract motion piece that explains nothing.

## Watch for
- Pace: understanding needs time. Hold a diagram until its point has landed (reading time plus a beat), and
  shorten elsewhere.
- Order: the viewer's model is built in sequence; a step that depends on a later one confuses everything after it.
- Narration: Fish is allowed for narration over the picture; a person on camera reads warmer when there is one.

Rubric weight: 2 and 3 decide it; 1 is the question the piece answers; 4 is the takeaway restated or the next step.
Add the learner as a fifth viewer: where do they lose the thread?
