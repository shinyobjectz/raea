# Refine: typeset

Type on a video is read once, moving, on a small screen, over pictures, against the clock. It carries information,
hierarchy and the voice. Improve it inside the piece's direction; replacing the faces is a direction change.

## Assess (answer each with a frame)
- **Roles.** Which jobs does type do here: hook, emphasis, caption, label, source or legal, the ask? Can each be
  told apart at a glance from size, weight, case and colour role, without reading?
- **Faces.** Which faces, and does each do a job only it can? Do they belong to BRAND.md and DIRECTION.md, or are
  they unexamined defaults? Two voices at most, usually.
- **Size and legibility.** Every role meets the floor at the frame's width, against the actual pixels behind it.
- **Breaks.** Do caption groups break at phrases, where the speaker breathes? Does a line ever end on a weak word
  ("the", "and", "of") or split a name or number?
- **Time.** Is each group on screen long enough to read (about 0.3 s a word, 1 s minimum)?
- **Consistency.** Same role, same treatment, in every beat.

## Set the system
Before editing, state: the roles, the contrast between them (a clear step in size or weight between adjacent
roles, never a near-miss), the faces and weights, and the caption treatment (group size, case, how the spoken
word is lit, what keeps it legible over footage: a shadow, a plate, a scrim). Use the fewest roles and faces that
make the hierarchy unmistakable.

## Apply
- Size from the frame: a 1080-wide phone frame reads far bigger type than a screen does; captions and hooks are
  large by design, labels smaller but above the floor.
- Emphasis on the word that carries meaning, not the loudest word; one way of marking it, held across the piece.
- Light text on dark or busy footage needs a little more weight and spacing than dark text on light.
- Uppercase reads as volume: a choice for some roles, not the default for all.
- Tight leading for display lines, more for anything longer than a few words.
- Let the layout wrap and size (the components, the brand's text classes); give every text box a width so it
  wraps where you mean.

## Verify
Roles recognisable with the words blurred; every group readable at phone size in time; the type belongs to this
brand and this direction. Check with `raea check` and stills.
