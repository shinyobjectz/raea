# Harmon: the story circle

Dan Harmon's story circle, written up in his posts on story structure for Channel 101 and used across his series. A
circle in eight steps, halved by the line between the familiar and the unfamiliar. In our words:

1. **You**: a character in a zone of comfort.
2. **Need**: but they want something.
3. **Go**: they enter an unfamiliar situation,
4. **Search**: adapt to it,
5. **Find**: get what they wanted,
6. **Take**: pay a heavy price for it,
7. **Return**: go back to the familiar,
8. **Change**: having changed.

Its strength is the sixth step: getting what you wanted costs something. Its shape repeats, which is why it suits
episodes and game loops.

## The lens (`harmon`)

Stages: `you`, `need`, `go`, `search`, `find` (the want met, or the high point), `take` (the first fall after it),
`return`, `change`.

It finds: no step into the unfamiliar (warn), a want got at no cost (warn), a circle that closes with no need met or
lie dropped (warn), an ending at the level it began (note).

Reach for it on series, sitcoms, and a game's core loop. Most ads won't pay the price step, and shouldn't be held to it.
