The direct render (RAEA_DIRECT=1): edits without 3D rendered through gpuview's pool and kernels (composite.k ->
nv12buf.k -> a ring of 3 readbacks in flight) with no Bevy schedules. UGC edit, 738 frames, 1080x1920, M4, Apple
decode and VideoToolbox encode, guarded, 2026-10-14, load ~4 (the orchestrator's build had just finished).

Exact: hash-only renders, two runs and one shuffled run: 738/738 frames byte-identical to the engine's GPU path
(examples/out/ref/gpu-apple-a.txt). The first try differed on the zoom 1.18 clips (147 frames): gpuview read row values
as f64 where the engine's World holds f32 (1.18 -> 1.17999995), moving the 16.16 source map; gpuview now rounds slot
inputs through f32 as the engine does.

The ceiling is the encoder: VideoToolbox H.264 alone, 300 frames of the take pushed as NV12 from memory, 8 Mbps:
212.1 fps, 212.0 fps (raea-media encode apple ... --nv12). The direct render runs at it. The encoder on its own thread
didn't raise it (its queue stays full: 194, 208, 148 fps, under background load); it stays, as it frees the loop the day
the encoder is faster.
Without the encoder (hash-only, minus the hashing's 3.16 ms): ~2.7 ms per frame, ~370 fps, of which decode+upload ~1.9.
The GPU is never waited on (wait 0.00 ms): composite + NV12 + copy finish while the CPU prepares the next frame.

Pool budget (engine::pool_budget: RAEA_POOL_MB, else 1 GiB on the Mac, 256 MiB on iOS/Android): at 256 MB the engine
and the direct render give the same 738 hashes, with the same 929 uploads (a render in order reuses frames only within
a few frames); peak footprint 628 MB (engine) and 614 MB (direct) vs 1431 MB at 1 GiB.
