# Storynomics: the customer is the hero

Robert McKee and Thomas Gerace, *Storynomics: Story-Driven Marketing in the Post-Advertising World* (2018). In our
words: a brand story is told about the customer, not the brand. The customer's life has a core value out of balance
(time, confidence, safety); the brand is the mentor or the gift that helps them act; the customer makes the decisive
move; the value is restored. A brand that's the hero of its own ad tells a story no one asked for.

## The lens (`storynomics`)

Stages: `at-stake` (the customer's value going wrong), `the-gift` (where the brand first acts), `the-act` (the
customer's decisive move), `restored`.

It finds: no customer at the centre (error), the brand making the decisive move instead of the customer (error), a
brand that never appears as mentor or gift (warn), and a value that doesn't end above its lowest point (error).

Reach for it on every brand film and ad, beside `pas` for short ones and `bernays` when it makes a claim.
